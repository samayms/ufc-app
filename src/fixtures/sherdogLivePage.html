UFC Oklahoma City ‘Du Plessis vs. Usman’ play-by-play, results & round scoring

Dione Barbosa (125) vs. Anna Melisano (126)

Round 1

Now the curtain-jerker, the women’s flyweight division will open up this event in Oklahoma City. Melisano (6-1, 0-0 UFC) will introduce herself to the promotion off of some slick stoppage wins under the Fury FC banner. She will try to resist the will of “The Witch” Barbosa (9-4, 3-2 UFC), who is sure to cast something or other before the match ends. The magician in command of the cage will be referee Kevin Nix, who will not hesitate to Wingardium Leviosa up in this beast if need be.

The ladies touch gloves to greet one another, with Melisano marching her way directly to the center of the cage behind a couple front kicks. Barbosa swats them all away and rushes in to tie Melisano up, using her level change to set up a body lock and potential throw, but Melisano keeps her balance on early attempts. Barbosa succeeds in wrestling Melisano to the mat, landing directly on top and establishing herself in mount for a moment. Melisano drags her back to her guard and throws her legs up high, but the slick Brazilian slips around to take the back in a hurry. Barbosa gets both hooks in and starts to threaten, before realizing she has a better path to success by retaking mount. Melisano keeps pushing off with her knees and feet to get any modicum of space, but Barbosa is all over her with heavy top pressure and submission setups aplenty.

Melisano stays defensive to stop the subs from going anywhere, until Barbosa postures up and starts clubbing her with ground-and-pound. Barbosa uses the moment to turn to an arm-triangle choke, and she follows a turning Melisano while cranking her neck awkwardly to threaten all the way through. Barbosa grabs hold of a neck crank and just twists Melisano’s head to the side painfully, and Melisano grits it out but is still fully controlled from behind. Melisano explodes back to her feet, and Barbosa welcomes this and leaps back upright to transform herself into a malicious backpack and snatch hold of a standing rear-naked choke grip with her legs around her foe’s waist. Melisano spits out her mouthpiece to find a little extra space to breathe, but it is to no avail. The forearm is under her chin, and Melisano tries to punch frantically behind her head while suffocating. Barbosa adjusts the grip and it is now a matter of time. Melisano elects to tap out before going out on her shield, although she did appear to think about it for a spell. It was not the best of debuts for Melisano, who shakes her head and gives it up to the victorious “Witch” who has enchanted the crowd with her quick tapout victory.

The Official Result

Dione Barbosa def. Anna Melisano R1 4:04 via Submission (Rear-Naked Choke)

Alden Coria (126) vs. Stewart Nicoll (126)

Round 1

Fresh off a win in his home city of Houston a few months ago, Coria (12-3, 1 NC; 2-0 UFC) crosses the northern border of Texas to go to its hat on top of Oklahoma. He draws Australia’s own Nicoll (8-3, 0-3 UFC), who can’t seem to buy a win let alone earn one in the Octagon to date. Referee Kevin Nix will keep tabs on the flyweights as they cruise at top speed, touching gloves before impact.

Nicoll bounces on his heels as he circles around the cage, with chants of “USA” raining down against him right off the bat. Coria takes to the center of the Octagon and flashes out a jab a few times, still looking for his range and timing. He nails Nicoll with a crisp uppercut nearly as solid as the one in the previous match, and the Aussie tanks it and has to blink it out. Coria walks him down and jacks him in the jaw with a right hand, and Nicoll turns tail and runs so he can reset. Nicoll settles himself down and jabs, but the moment he does, Coria counters with another uppercut right on the button. Nicoll lunges behind a left hand, and Coria shrugs it off completely before getting back into Nicoll’s face. Nicoll’s head movement keeps him relatively safe, but Coria is still scoring on him practically at will. Nicoll looks for a right hand over the top, and Coria jams his foot in the stomach in response.

Coria beats Nicoll to the punch with a jab, and Nicoll responds with a takedown shot. Coria backs himself to the wall and defends with a guillotine choke, so Nicoll wrenches him away from it and drags him to the floor with a body lock. Coria fights his way back to his feet without taking any damage, and he breaks off from a clinch to corner his foe against the fencing. Nicoll punches his way out and is met with a crisp right hand that gets his attention, his left eye starting to swell. Nicoll bites down on his mouthpiece and throws hands, spinning his way into a back kick that at least partially grazes the cup. Nix looks to Coria, who does not seem concerned. Nicoll blasts through the hips with a double-leg entry, dragging the Houstonian to the mat. Coria wall-walks in short order, and he hits a switch and turns Nicoll around before falling off the back. They reset in kickboxing range, and Nicoll looks to seal the round with one final takedown that he completes.

Sherdog Scores

Jay Pettry scores the round: 10-9 Coria
Ben Duffy scores the round: 10-9 Coria
Tyler Treese scores the round: 10-9 Coria

Round 2

Coria comes out of his corner jabbing away, rolling with a counter overhand right while backing Nicoll away. Coria lets rip a front kick that slams square into the cup, and Nix calls time as the fans boo the Australian for some reason. Nicoll asks for 10 seconds, and Nix of course obliges him as he has five minutes to recover if needed. They clap hands to get back after it, and Coria kicks his man in the front leg showing he will not blunt his kicking arsenal because of the accidental foul. Nicoll meets him with a few low kicks of his own and a front kick to end the short string of them, his overhand right after going wide. Coria has his jab stuck in Nicoll’s face, and he gets nailed with a calf kick and almost goes down because of it. The Texan gathers himself and pecks away at his opponent, with Nicoll shelling up awkwardly in the center of the cage before shooting for a takedown that does not come. Coria has his uppercut primed and ready, although his pace has diminished to a degree two minutes in. Coria takes issue with this characterization and lays it on Nicoll, punishing him with a flurry of punches until Nicoll shoots for a takedown. Coria drops down and intercepts a takedown to snatch up an anaconda choke, and Nicoll turns out of it to attempt another shot from a different angle.

Nicoll clasps his hands around his opponent’s backside and lifts him in the air like a Country Breakfast-fed Matt Hughes, slamming him to the ground in the center of the cage with style points. Nicoll wraps up a crucifix by hooking Coria’s right arm beneath his legs with Coria on his knees, and he rolls him over to take Coria’s back. As Coria twists and turns, Nicoll follows him with a bulldog choke until Coria slips out of his grip. Coria whizzes around to take Nicoll’s back and bully him to the fencing, with Nicoll controlling his left arm for a potential kimura sweep as a huge grin spreads across his face. That expression changes when Coria gets him down from behind, riding out with back control as he slugs Nicoll under his armpit.

Sherdog Scores

Jay Pettry scores the round: 10-9 Coria
Ben Duffy scores the round: 10-9 Coria
Tyler Treese scores the round: 10-9 Coria

Round 3

The 125ers high-five to open the last round, and Coria starts the round like the first two by walking his man down and busting him in the face with jabs and follow-up punches. Nicoll ducks down into a head kick, and he wears it well but is still taking shots. The Aussie throws back, but Coria does not seem to respect his power as he steps in with a slick left hook around the guard. Nicoll sways and dodges, and Coria sees this and times an uppercut as the commentary team practically calls for the strike. As Coria loads up, Nicoll times a double and drags the Texan to the mat. Nicoll is solely concerned with control over submission or damage, and Coria scoots his way back to the fence and tries to escape. Nicoll flirts with an inverted triangle choke while Coria is scrambling, and the two work back to their feet. Coria keeps right on him working the head and body, and Nicoll’s movement is not keeping him as safe as he wishes.

Coria lands again and again, and Nicoll tries to go for one home run response. The swings are big but do not land as often, and his takedown shot is met with a brabo choke as he rolls his man over with the grip still locked in. Nicoll grits it out and explodes to turn to his knees and free himself from the submission, with Coria now trying to isolate Nicoll’s right arm for his own crucifix as if to say “two can play that game.” Nicoll twists well enough to pursue a single and push Coria to the fence, and Coria snatches up a guillotine choke and jumps guard for it. Nicoll smoothly rolls through it to stand back up, and upon another shot, this time, Coria has the power guillotine tight as a drum. Nicoll slumps over as Coria puts him down on his back, and the grip falls off as time elapses.

Sherdog Scores

Jay Pettry scores the round: 10-9 Coria (30-27 Coria)
Ben Duffy scores the round: 10-9 Coria (30-27 Coria)
Tyler Treese scores the round: 10-9 Coria (30-27 Coria)

The Official Result

Alden Coria def. Stewart Nicoll via Unanimous Decision (30-27, 30-27, 29-28)

Dricus Du Plessis (185.5) vs. Kamaru Usman (186)

Round 1

Major stakes loom for the headliner of this Oklahoma City showdown. Former 185-pound champ Du Plessis (23-3, 9-1 UFC) will face a tough stylistic matchup, the type like previous opponent Khamzat Chimaev who handed him his first loss in the Octagon with all the wrestling. Unfortunately for “DDP,” Usman (21-4, 16-3 UFC) has all the wrestling, and appears to have adjusted to his middleweight frame. Stocks could rise and plummet significantly after the dust settles, and referee Kerry Hatley knows this one is a meaningful endeavor. Intense but respectful, the two men stoically bump fists to seal the cage for this main attraction. It’s on with the show.

Du Plessis comes out of his corner a big jittery as usual, giving Usman strange reads as he pushes out a jab and works on the former welterweight’s plant leg. Usman’s responses are all parried early, with Du Plessis at his preferred range pecking with low kicks and a front kick for good measure. Usman jabs him in the stomach and eats a right hand over the top, eager to trade hands. The audience is split on their support for the fighters, with alternating chants of “DDP” and “Usman” echoing through the Paycom Center. Usman is largely stuck in first gear with jabs to the chest, and Du Plessis offers back two overhand rights. Usman fakes a level change and sticks around a bit too long, and “Stillknocks” bashes him behind the ear and disrupts his equilibrium momentarily. Just as Usman shakes out the cobwebs, Du Plessis stings him again.

Du Plessis plants hard calf kicks on his opponent, and Usman is visibly wincing when absorbing them. The former welterweight kingpin offers back single strikes, while Du Plessis is putting two or three together at a time. Usman steps into the pocket to trade, landing once or twice before he is pushed back. Du Plessis skips a right hand off the temple, and Usman clips “DDP” a few times. The South African uses his jab to bang into Usman any time Usman advances, having found his range four minutes in. Just then, Usman crashes forward to tie him up in a clinch. In the process, Usman lifts up a knee that bounces into the cup. Hatley calls time to allow Du Plessis to recover, and resets them in a neutral position. Du Plessis thanks him for this by kicking Usman upside the head, and he sways back from one jab only to get drilled with another. Usman does have some power coming up in weight, but Du Plessis’ accuracy is high and his impacts matter. Du Plessis concludes the round by pounding Usman in the ribs with his shin.

Sherdog Scores

Jay Pettry scores the round: 10-9 Du Plessis
Ben Duffy scores the round: 10-9 Du Plessis
Tyler Treese scores the round: 10-9 Du Plessis

Round 2

The gloves are touched to start out the round, and Usman comes out swinging. Du Plessis slides back to let the fists soar past him, only to step back in and split the guard with a piston-like jab. “Stillknocks” connects with two head kicks, and he times a spectacular knee when Usman ducks down to put “The Nigerian Nightmare” on baby deer legs. Usman somehow wills himself back to balance again, and Du Plessis jams him up with another powerful knee, this one to the solar plexus. Usman appears fresh again, bobbing and weaving, and he has his guard up to block a head kick but the sheer impact moves him. Usman shoots for a double, and Du Plessis knees him right in the chin to shake him up again. In part due to the knee, Du Plessis is able to break up Usman’s attack just enough to slide out the side and get away. Stepping in with a combination and changing stances midway through, Du Plessis has Usman guessing at this point, and the guesses are largely wrong. Du Plessis sells out for an overhand right and gets countered, but his knee to the chin seemingly cannot miss.

Usman smiles as he displays that his chin is not fully cracked by Leon Edwards, and he lets loose a right hand. Du Plessis lets Usman lumber towards him to intercept him with a big right hand on the eye socket. The fist was closed and it was a legal punch, and the replay officials verify it after a brief pause. Du Plessis stalks down his prey, hurting him with a combination and sending him staggering back to his seat. Usman works his way back up to go after Du Plessis, tying up the former middleweight champ but only for a few seconds. Du Plessis puts a one-two on the cheek, and Usman reels and tries to throw back. Du Plessis swings himself down to his knees, and he pushes off before Usman can take advantage of it. Du Plessis grabs the back of Usman’s head and cracks him with a knee, buzzing the tower with a head kick shortly thereafter. Du Plessis leaps into his attacks, having developed some swelling around Usman’s left eye. Du Plessis further adds to it with a sharp one-two, whipping a right hand at the damaged eye to further work on it. The round ends, and Usman wipes some blood away from his eyebrow.

Sherdog Scores

Jay Pettry scores the round: 10-9 Du Plessis
Ben Duffy scores the round: 10-9 Du Plessis
Tyler Treese scores the round: 10-9 Du Plessis

Round 3

Usman moves directly to the center of the cage as he absorbs a kick to the stomach, and he fakes a takedown to draw out a reaction. Du Plessis does not bite nearly as much as Usman wishes, and instead stays relatively composed with his steady activity. Usman reaches him with a right hand, and he still gets chinned with a knee. Du Plessis reaches over a jab with a right hand, and Usman’s right to the body forces out an involuntary grunt from the South African. Du Plessis replies with a speedy head kick, and Usman protects himself right in time. Du Plessis goes to the body with a jump kick, landing so he can plant his same shin on the side right after it. Usman loads up on his right, faking for a takedown as Du Plessis nearly falls for it.

Usman offers up his level change twice more, and Du Plessis just misses with a step-in knee. Doubling up with a jab and a right hand, Du Plessis reaches his target and only takes a blocked right hand back. Usman’s shot leads to Du Plessis dropping to his knees, and Du Plessis bounces up and has his knee primed and ready to drive into Usman’s chin. Du Plessis sneaks his way in with a one-two that Usman does not see coming, his eye closing to an extent. Du Plessis puts a one-two on the chin and meets Usman shooting in on his hips. Usman lifts Du Plessis off the ground and puts him down, and within a second, Du Plessis is back upright again. Nevertheless, Usman is encouraged and smiles. He lowers himself down and takes another knee on the nose, still flirting with this fake takedown to get reactions. Du Plessis puts one final combination on his foe’s face to wrap up the round.

Sherdog Scores

Jay Pettry scores the round: 10-9 Du Plessis
Ben Duffy scores the round: 10-9 Du Plessis
Tyler Treese scores the round: 10-9 Du Plessis

Round 4

Reaching the championship rounds, Usman once more puts himself in the center of the Octagon offering up offense. His jabs to the body are met with a heavier body kick, and he responds with a jabbing front kick to the breadbasket. Hatley warns Du Plessis for keeping his hands open, and this does not stop him from keeping his fingers outstretched towards his foe. Du Plessis gets behind his jab, dancing away from most of Usman’s replies. Usman drops low to get hold of a single, and Du Plessis springs away, bounces off the fence and skims the shiny dome of “The Nigerian Nightmare” with his foot. Usman starts to put more oomph behind his swings, while Du Plessis accurately works him. Du Plessis blasts Usman in the raised guard with a kick, and the sheer force of the blow wobbles the former welterweight. Du Plessis hurls another to the same spot, and Usman blocks it and retreats. Du Plessis walks him down with long punches, setting up another head kick as Usman can only raise his shield. Du Plessis cracks his man with ferocious right hands, and Usman tries to take him down.

Du Plessis shuts down what is a much feebler takedown effort than before, forcing Usman to stand so he can work his magic. Du Plessis wraps a right hand around the ear and staggers Usman for the umpteenth time, who proceeds to try to clinch up. Du Plessis has none of it and pushes off, keeping his jab and twos behind it. Usman just misses with a haymaker that would have knocked down a bull moose, and Du Plessis acts like he will go high with a kick and instead digs it beneath the armpit. Du Plessis plants a crisp one-two on the chin, and Usman’s wide overhand right is nowhere near getting “DDP” back. Usman puts everything he has into a one-two, and it is now Du Plessis who has to take a moment to clear his vision. Usman uses the opportunity to kick him twice in the chest, and Du Plessis reacts by pitching another head kick. The round comes to an end.

Sherdog Scores

Jay Pettry scores the round: 10-9 Du Plessis
Ben Duffy scores the round: 10-9 Du Plessis
Tyler Treese scores the round: 10-9 Du Plessis

Round 5

The boo birds have left the building, as the raucous crowd gives it up for the fighters. Du Plessis keeps his guard up early, expecting the offense from Usman that does indeed come. Du Plessis keeps a wide berth and kicks at the end of his leg, maximizing his range while prohibiting Usman from striking back. Usman frowns when kicked in the gut, and his counters hit nothing but air. Du Plessis lands a staggered one-two, delaying the right hand behind it as Usman drops his guard just enough. Du Plessis thumps up the midsection with another kick and plants two fists on the chin behind it, and Usman mightily throws back to knock out the wind beside them. Du Plessis whips an inside low kick to the cup, and Hatley calls time as Usman grimaces. Du Plessis apologizes for the inadvertent foul, and the two resume after about 45 seconds. Du Plessis reintroduces himself with his favorite head kick, and he sets up a stance switch to rock Usman badly. Usman’s legs are barely beneath him as Du Plessis lays into him with two more head kicks.

Usman’s recoverability is otherworldly given how many times he has been hurt in this fight, and he puts everything he has into power punches in hopes of performing a miracle. When he stings Du Plessis with a right hand, Du Plessis shoots for a takedown and is shut down hard. When both back upright, suddenly it is Usman who looks fresh while Du Plessis is fading. Usman blocks the kicks swinging at him and boots “DDP” in the stomach and front leg. Du Plessis retreats with his hands high, and Usman jabs him hard enough to nearly dislodge his gumshield. Du Plessis kicks his man in the stomach, and Usman’s foot may have bumped him in the cup. Nothing is called, and Usman nails Du Plessis with a right hand that has him in trouble. Usman tries to advance but is pushed back from the jabs of the South African. Du Plessis kicks the pectoral and uppercuts Usman coming in, shooting in when Usman lashes out. Time almost up, Usman lets go with a final barrage of punches, and Du Plessis comes up top and crashes his fist into Usman’s temple. The competitive, yet likely one-sided on the scorecards, matchup comes to a close, and the crowd gives it up for the two gladiators who put it all on the line tonight.

When the scores are read, Usman appears surprised at the tallies so lopsided for “DDP.” In his triumphant post-fight interview, Du Plessis gives it up for Usman for giving him a terrific fight. He bashes Khamzat Chimaev without naming him, saying that Chimaev laying on top of him was not what fighters should do. He offers ample praise for his foe, while also informing the audience that he is “Dricus 2.0” who claims he has trained his mind to stay composed. He wants his belt back, and with two wins over the current champion, it may be hard to determine a worthier contender other than perhaps Nassourdine Imavov. When that next middleweight championship excursion goes down, we will be here for it. We hope you are too.

Sherdog Scores

Jay Pettry scores the round: 10-9 Du Plessis (50-45 Du Plessis)
Ben Duffy scores the round: 10-9 Usman (49-46 Du Plessis)
Tyler Treese scores the round: 10-9 Usman (49-46 Du Plessis)

The Official Result

Dricus Du Plessis def. Kamaru Usman via Unanimous Decision (50-45, 49-46, 49-46)
